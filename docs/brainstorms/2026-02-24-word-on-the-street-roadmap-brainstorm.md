# Word on the Street — Roadmap Brainstorm

## What We're Building
A newsroom-style pulse system that surfaces what’s moving right now. The initial core use case is AI tooling and engineering decisions, but the product has a wide surface area: purchase intelligence, local pulse, culture trends, and shareable briefings.

## Why This Approach
We’re capturing ideas by horizon to avoid over-designing. “Now” focuses on the core audience and the smallest artifact that proves value. “Next” adds distribution and packaging. “Later” expands to broader audiences and local or cultural beats.

## Now (0–3 months)
- **AI tooling pulse**: Daily/weekly briefings for AI tools, agent frameworks, evals, and platform shifts.
- **Decision support**: “What’s moving and why” for tool selection (signals + verification trails).
- **Briefing artifact v1**: A clean, shareable report that can be sent to teammates.
- **Signals over search**: Focus on multi-source signals vs personalized feeds.

## Next (3–6 months)
- **Product intelligence reports**: “What are people saying about X product?” including common issues, firmware problems, and sentiment.
- **Shareable one-file briefing**: Single-file report bundle (HTML + CSS + JS) that can be shared like a PDF.
- **Email delivery**: Deliver briefings by email with interactive sections (expand/collapse, filter sources).
- **Interactive report UI**: Lightweight web view with source trails and inline verification steps.

## Later (6–12 months)
- **Local pulse**: What’s happening in your city or neighborhood this weekend (geo-targeted briefings).
- **Culture & trend radar**: Fashion, music, events, and micro-trends across platforms.
- **General audience modes**: Teen-friendly and parent-friendly briefing formats.
- **Personalized beats**: Choose beats without algorithmic personalization; opt-in topic “desks.”

## Key Decisions
- Keep the core product framed as a newsroom pulse engine, not a recap tool.
- Preserve the anti-algorithmic ethos even as personalization or local filters appear.
- Use “briefings as artifacts” as a distribution strategy for sharing and trust.

## Open Questions
- Should “product intelligence” be a named sub-brand or a feature mode?
- How much automation is acceptable before it feels like an algorithmic feed?
- What verification trails are essential for trust in general consumer segments?
